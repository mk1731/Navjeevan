Admission Process – Direct Second Year (Lateral Entry)

  1. Admissions are conducted through CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra.
  2. Candidates must apply online for Direct Second Year (DSE) Engineering.
  3. Merit list is prepared based on Diploma / B.Sc. (Science) performance.
  4. Seat allotment is done through Centralized Admission Process (CAP).
  5. Selected candidates must report to the institute for admission confirmation.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must have passed Diploma in Engineering / Technology from a recognized institute.
  • OR passed B.Sc. Degree from a recognized university with Mathematics at 12th level.
  • Minimum percentage as prescribed by CET Cell / DBATU.
  • Eligibility and reservation norms as per Government of Maharashtra.

Document Requirements

  • Diploma / B.Sc. Mark Sheets (All Semesters)
  • Diploma / Degree Passing Certificate
  • 10th & 12th Mark Sheets
  • Leaving / Transfer Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Caste Certificate & Caste Validity (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs

Fee Structure (Indicative)

Fee structure for Direct Second Year Engineering admissions is approved by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA), Government of Maharashtra.

  • Tuition Fee – As per FRA approval
  • Development Fee – As applicable
  • University & Examination Fees

Exact fees will be notified at the time of admission.

Rules & Regulations
