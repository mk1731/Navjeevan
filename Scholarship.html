Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Government Scholarship Schemes for Diploma & B.Tech Students

Navjeevan Education Society’s Polytechnic & College of Engineering facilitates various Government of Maharashtra scholarship schemes for eligible Diploma and B.Tech students through the MahaDBT (MahaDITP) Portal.


Category-wise Scholarship Schemes

General / Minority

  • Minority Scholarship (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Jain, Buddhist)
  • Merit-cum-Means Scholarship

OBC Category

  • Post Matric Scholarship (OBC)
  • Tuition Fee & Exam Fee (OBC)

EWS Category

  • Education Fee Reimbursement (EWS)

VJ / NT Category

  • Post Matric Scholarship (VJNT)
  • Fee Reimbursement Scheme

SC Category

  • Post Matric Scholarship (SC)
  • Tuition Fee & Exam Fee (SC)

ST Category

  • Post Matric Scholarship (ST)
  • Maintenance Allowance

Application Process & Required Documents

Scholarship Application Process

  1. Register on MahaDBT Portal
  2. Login using Aadhaar-based credentials
  3. Fill personal, academic & income details
  4. Select the appropriate scholarship scheme
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Submit application and track status

Required Documents

  • Caste Certificate
  • Caste Validity Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Previous Year Marksheet
  • Admission Receipt / Bonafide Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Account (linked with Aadhaar)

Apply Through MahaDBT Portal

All scholarship applications must be submitted online through the official Government of Maharashtra portal.
