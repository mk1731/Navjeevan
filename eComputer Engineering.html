About the Course

B.Tech in Computer Engineering focuses on core computing principles including programming, data structures, algorithms, software engineering, operating systems, databases, and modern computing technologies.

Key Areas

  • Programming & Software Development
  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Database Management Systems
  • Operating Systems
  • Computer Networks & Cyber Security

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics or Diploma in Engineering, as per DTE Maharashtra and DBATU norms.

Duration

The programme is a 4-year full-time B.Tech degree course divided into 8 semesters.

Curriculum

The DBATU-prescribed curriculum includes theory, laboratory work, internships, mini-projects, and an industry-oriented final year project to prepare students for professional careers.
