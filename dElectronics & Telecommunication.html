About the Course

Diploma in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, embedded technology, networking fundamentals, and modern telecommunication systems.

Specializations

  • Electronic Devices & Circuits
  • Analog & Digital Communication
  • Embedded Systems
  • Microcontrollers & Microprocessors
  • Wireless & Optical Communication

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed SSC (10th Standard) from a recognized board as per MSBTE / DTE Maharashtra norms.

Duration

The programme is a 3-year full-time diploma course divided into 6 semesters.

Curriculum

The MSBTE-prescribed curriculum includes theory, laboratory work, mini-projects, industrial training, and communication skills required for the electronics and telecom industry.
