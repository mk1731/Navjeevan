Admission Process – Direct Second Year

  1. Admissions are conducted as per the guidelines of DTE Maharashtra for Lateral Entry.
  2. Candidates must apply through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP).
  3. Merit list is prepared based on Diploma / ITI / 12th (Science / Vocational) marks.
  4. Seats are allotted through centralized rounds.
  5. Selected candidates must report to the institute for document verification and admission confirmation.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must have passed ITI (2 years) or 12th Science / Vocational stream.
  • Eligibility as prescribed by MSBTE and DTE Maharashtra.
  • Candidate must be an Indian National.
  • Reservation policy applicable as per Government norms.

Document Requirements

  • 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • ITI / 12th Marksheet & Certificate
  • School Leaving / Transfer Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate & Caste Validity (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs

Fee Structure (Indicative)

Fees are prescribed by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA), Government of Maharashtra, for Direct Second Year admissions.

  • Tuition Fee – As per FRA approval
  • Development Fee – As applicable
  • Examination Fee – As per MSBTE norms
  • Other Charges – Library, Laboratory, and Student Activities

Note: Exact fee details will be announced at the time of admission.

Rules & Regulations

  • Students must adhere to MSBTE academic rules.
  • Minimum attendance requirements must be fulfilled.
  • Ragging is strictly prohibited as per Government regulations.
  • Institute reserves the right to modify rules as required.
  • All students must follow the institute’s code of conduct.
