About the Course

B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, signal processing, embedded systems, wireless communication, and modern telecommunication technologies.

Key Areas

  • Analog & Digital Communication
  • Embedded Systems & IoT
  • Microprocessors & Microcontrollers
  • Wireless & Optical Communication
  • Signal Processing

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics or Diploma in Engineering, as per DTE Maharashtra and DBATU norms.

Duration

The programme is a 4-year full-time B.Tech degree course divided into 8 semesters.

Curriculum

The DBATU curriculum emphasizes core electronics theory, hands-on laboratory work, industrial training, internships, and project-based learning aligned with telecom industry needs.
