Admission Process

  1. Admissions are carried out as per the guidelines of the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra.
  2. Candidates must register online through the official admission portal.
  3. Merit list will be prepared based on SSC marks.
  4. Seat allotment will be done through centralized admission rounds.
  5. Selected candidates must confirm admission by reporting to the institute.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must have passed SSC (10th Standard) from a recognized board.
  • Minimum passing marks as prescribed by MSBTE / DTE Maharashtra.
  • Candidate must be an Indian National.
  • Reservation criteria applicable as per Government norms.

Document Requirements

  • SSC Marksheet
  • SSC Passing Certificate
  • School Leaving Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate & Caste Validity (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs

Fee Structure (Indicative)

The fee structure is as per the approval of the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA), Government of Maharashtra.

  • Tuition Fee – As per FRA approval
  • Development Fee – Applicable as per norms
  • Examination Fee – As prescribed by MSBTE
  • Other Charges – Library, Laboratory, and Student Activities

Note: Exact fee details will be notified during admission.

Rules & Regulations

  • Students must maintain minimum attendance as prescribed by MSBTE.
  • Discipline and code of conduct must be strictly followed.
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited.
  • Institute reserves the right to amend rules as per academic requirements.
  • Students must comply with examination and internal assessment rules.
