Admission Process

  1. Admissions are conducted through CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra.
  2. Candidates must appear for MHT-CET or JEE (Main) as applicable.
  3. Online registration through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP).
  4. Merit list and seat allotment based on entrance score and preferences.
  5. Selected candidates must report to the institute for confirmation.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must have passed 12th Science (HSC) or equivalent.
  • Physics and Mathematics compulsory subjects.
  • Valid MHT-CET / JEE (Main) score.
  • Eligibility as prescribed by DBATU and Government of Maharashtra.

Document Requirements

  • 10th & 12th Mark Sheets
  • 12th Passing Certificate
  • School Leaving Certificate
  • MHT-CET / JEE Score Card
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Caste Certificate & Validity (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs
