About the Course

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering focuses on the theoretical foundations of computation, advanced programming concepts, software systems, algorithms, and emerging areas of computer science that drive modern technology.

Key Areas

  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Operating Systems
  • Database Systems
  • Software Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics or Diploma in Engineering, as per DTE Maharashtra and DBATU norms.

Duration

The programme is a 4-year full-time B.Tech degree course divided into 8 semesters.

Curriculum

The DBATU-prescribed curriculum blends theoretical concepts, laboratory-based learning, internships, research exposure, and a comprehensive final year project.
