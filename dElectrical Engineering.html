About the Course

Diploma in Electrical Engineering focuses on electrical machines, power systems, electrical installations, control systems, and industrial electrical applications.

Specializations

  • Electrical Machines & Drives
  • Power Generation, Transmission & Distribution
  • Industrial Electrical Systems
  • Control Systems & PLC Basics
  • Renewable Energy Systems

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed SSC (10th Standard) from a recognized board as per MSBTE / DTE Maharashtra norms.

Duration

The programme is a 3-year full-time diploma course divided into 6 semesters.

Curriculum

The MSBTE-prescribed curriculum includes electrical theory, hands-on laboratory experiments, industrial training, and project-based learning aligned with industry needs.
