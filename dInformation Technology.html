About the Course

Diploma in Information Technology focuses on IT infrastructure, networking, web systems, databases, software applications, and emerging digital technologies used in industry.

Specializations

  • Web Development & Web Applications
  • Networking & System Administration
  • Database Management Systems
  • Information Security Basics
  • Cloud & Internet Technologies

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed SSC (10th Standard) from a recognized board as per MSBTE / DTE Maharashtra norms.

Duration

The programme is a 3-year full-time diploma course divided into 6 semesters.

Curriculum

The curriculum prescribed by MSBTE covers theory, practicals, projects, and hands-on skill development aligned with IT industry requirements.
