About the Course

Diploma in Computer Engineering focuses on programming, databases, networking, software development, and modern computing technologies.

Specializations

  • Programming & Software Development
  • Web Technologies
  • Database Management Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Cyber Security Basics

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed SSC (10th Standard) from a recognized board as per MSBTE / DTE Maharashtra norms.

Duration

The programme is a 3-year full-time diploma course divided into 6 semesters.

Curriculum

The curriculum is prescribed by MSBTE and includes theory, practicals, projects, and skill-based learning.
