Building Strong
Foundations in Technical
Education Since 1995

Navjeevan Education Society’s Polytechnic was established in 1995–96 with approval from the Government of Maharashtra and DTE, and is affiliated to MSBTE, Mumbai, offering three-year Diploma programmes in engineering.

From the academic year 2025–26, the institute has introduced B.Tech degree programmes, affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), focusing on quality technical education and skill development.
