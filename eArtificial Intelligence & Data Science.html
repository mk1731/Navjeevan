About the Course

B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science focuses on intelligent systems, data analytics, machine learning, deep learning, and real-world problem solving using AI-driven technologies across industries.

Key Areas

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Data Science & Big Data Analytics
  • Deep Learning & Neural Networks
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision

Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must have passed HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics or Diploma in Engineering, as per DTE Maharashtra and DBATU norms.

Duration

The programme is a 4-year full-time B.Tech degree course divided into 8 semesters.

Curriculum

The DBATU-prescribed curriculum includes theory, laboratory work, internships, skill-based learning, research-oriented projects, and industry-aligned final year projects.
